How to work (pairs):
  1. Pick one real task from your team — something done often, by several people. Not a dream project.
  2. Go gate by gate. Read the yellow probing questions first, then type. Short answers are fine; vague answers are not.
  3. The page checks your answers like a review board would and raises red flags. Fix them or explain them.
  4. At the end: print or save your canvas, and copy the "challenge me" prompt into Abby for a tough second opinion.
The rule for today: if you cannot answer a question, that is the finding. Write "Don't know yet — ask <name>". That is exactly what real projects discover too late.
